DARREN & GAVIN WELCOMES YOU TO THE PRINCE OF WALES

Darren and Gavin are cousins with a shared passion for creating a welcoming, memorable experience for everyone who walks through the doors of The Prince of Wales. Together, they bring over 40 years of combined expertise to the pub and its accommodation, blending professionalism with genuine Cornish hospitality.

Gavin has spent more than 20 years in the hospitality industry, building a reputation for exceptional guest care, warm service, and an instinctive understanding of what makes a stay truly enjoyable. Darren brings his own 20+ years of management experience, ensuring everything runs smoothly behind the scenes so guests can relax, unwind, and make the most of their time in Falmouth.

Both are enthusiastic, hands on hosts who take pride in offering comfort, friendliness, and a great atmosphere. Whether guests are here for the lively nightlife, the coastal charm, or a cosy place to rest, Darren and Gavin are committed to making every stay at The Prince of Wales a great one.
