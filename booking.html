Stay at the Prince of Wales Pub – Your Base for Falmouth’s Best Nights and Bright Mornings

More than a place to sleep

At the Prince of Wales, we offer three comfortable, character-filled rooms perfectly placed for guests who want to experience everything Falmouth has to offer. With nightlife, live music, bars, restaurants, and shops just steps away, you’ll be right in the heart of the action. Our rooms are ideal for travellers who enjoy late nights, early adventures, and the vibrant atmosphere of the town. Because of our central location, the area can stay lively into the night — but we’ve got you covered. Complimentary earplugs are provided to help you settle in for a peaceful sleep whenever you’re ready to unwind. Whether you’re here for the beaches, the harbour, the culture, or the nightlife, the Prince of Wales gives you the perfect place to rest, recharge, and enjoy Falmouth your way.
